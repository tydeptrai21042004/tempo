LIVE-STREAM BENCHMARK

Quick Kaggle-compatible virtual-arrival run:
  ./run.sh stream --workdir reports/live_stream --frames 120 \
    --resolutions 512x512 --target-fps 30 --channels clean,jpeg70 \
    --wm-size 16 --pace-mode virtual

Wall-clock paced run:
  ./run.sh stream --workdir reports/live_stream_realtime --frames 120 \
    --resolutions 512x512 --target-fps 30 --channels clean,jpeg70 \
    --wm-size 16 --pace-mode realtime

Kaggle wrapper:
  ./kaggle.sh stream --workdir /kaggle/working/live_stream \
    --frames 120 --resolutions 512x512,1280x720 \
    --target-fps 30 --channels clean,jpeg70 --wm-size 16

Main outputs:
  ALL_STREAM_realtime_summary.csv
  ALL_STREAM_realtime_trace.csv
  ALL_STREAM_calibration.csv
  ALL_STREAM_run_manifest.json

DEW uses paper-style keyed differential-energy regions and requires at least
two 8x8 DCT blocks per bit. The stream benchmark therefore uses a 16x16
watermark (256 bits) by default. Existing 4096-bit image/video protocols are
left unchanged and do not silently reduce their payload.

Revision 1.1 notes:
  - virtual and realtime modes now both use a drop-oldest bounded queue;
  - on_time_completion_ratio is computed over all generated frames;
  - sustained_realtime_end_to_end requires complete processing, no drops,
    effective FPS at or above the target, and p95 end-to-end latency within
    the frame deadline;
  - proposal traces include fast/recovery extraction path, pilot score,
    selected synchronization candidate, shift, and integer-closure counters.
